Practical information

Everything ready for day one

Class Hours

Our intensive courses consist of 15 hours per week, with three flexible scheduling options to fit your lifestyle:

  • Morning (Mon–Fri): 9:00 – 12:10
  • Afternoon (Mon–Fri): 13:00 – 16:10
  • Afternoon (Mon–Thu): 13:00 – 17:00

MySEDA App

Use the MySEDA app to stay in control of your studies. From checking your class details and school activities to receiving real-time updates and requesting letters, managing your course has never been easier.

Coursebooks

Start your lessons fully prepared. For your convenience, all required coursebooks are available for purchase directly at the school reception. No need to search elsewhere—get the right materials and start learning from day one.

English Immersion

  • Experience Full Immersion: our school is a dedicated English-only zone to help you learn faster.
  • Commit to the Language: English is the only language spoken in our classrooms, creating a true immersion experience.
