Before applying, please read our Terms:

1. Enrolment and Payment

Your completed enrolment form must be submitted alongside full payment for the course. Please note that course fees do not include registration or examination fees unless specifically confirmed by SEDA in writing.

All Awarding Body Registration & Examination fees must be paid to the administration at the time of course payment. Participation in these fees is a mandatory requirement for enrolment; students who do not settle these fees will not be eligible for admission.

2. Academic Requirements

  • All students are required to complete a Placement Test to determine their English level.
  • Students must attend all scheduled English classes, participate in mock and final examinations, and submit written coursework as requested by their teachers.
  • For visa compliance, all full-time courses are delivered over a minimum of 15 hours per week, in accordance with Department of Justice (ISD) guidelines.

3. Contractual Agreement

By accepting an offer from SEDA and completing payment, a student enters into a formal contract with the College for the duration specified in the Letter of Enrolment (from commencement to completion date).

4. Timetable Adjustments

While we strive to maintain consistent schedules, SEDA reserves the right to adjust student timetables due to unforeseen circumstances. SEDA is not liable for any such changes, and no compensation or refunds will be provided in the event of a timetable modification.

5. Conduct and Visa Compliance

SEDA reserves the right to suspend or expel any student who fails to fulfill their visa requirements or engages in disruptive behavior. In such instances, no refund of fees will be granted.

6. Immigration and External Bodies

ISD/GNIB Regulations: International students are responsible for complying with all Irish Immigration (ISD) regulations. Further details are available from our administration team or the official ISD website.

Professional Institutes: Where applicable, students must obtain a certificate of eligibility from the relevant professional body and pay any external registration fees directly to that institution.
