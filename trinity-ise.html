Trinity ISE

Prove you have the skills for the real world! Trinity ISE is more than just a test; it's a qualification that shows you can interact naturally in English. It focuses on the integrated skills you actually need for university life and professional success.

At SEDA College, we prepare students for the Trinity ISE exams across all levels.

SEDA College is a registered Trinity exam centre, meaning our students enjoy the convenience of sitting their ISE exams right here at the school.

What an ISE certificate proves

An ISE certificate proves to universities and employers that the holder can use English effectively for study and work. It demonstrates that you can:

  • Engage in spontaneous conversation and express your own ideas and opinions with confidence during a one-to-one interview.
  • Synthesize information from multiple reading sources to create professional reports and essays.
  • Listen and take notes effectively, just as you would in a university lecture or a business meeting.
  • Adapt your language to different social and professional contexts, whether formal or informal.
  • Demonstrate academic literacy through tasks that mirror real-life university assignments.
