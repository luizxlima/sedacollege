MySEDA – Your Student App

Everything you need, at your fingertips

When you study at SEDA, everything you need is at your fingertips with MySEDA – your student app.

MySEDA is designed to make your life easier and help you stay on track during your time in Ireland. Instead of emailing or visiting the office, you can manage most things quickly and easily in one place.

With MySEDA, you can:

  • Check your attendance and stay on track with your course
  • Request important documents like attendance letters, visa letters, and certificates
  • View your timetable and college calendar
  • Receive important messages and updates from the college
  • Keep your personal information up to date

Using MySEDA helps you stay organised, informed, and in control of your learning. It also means you can get the support you need, when you need it, without waiting.

MySEDA is available on your phone, so you can access everything anytime, anywhere.
