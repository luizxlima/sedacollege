About TIE

Developed in Ireland by language professionals, TIE is a highly regarded international EFL exam. It is “learner-centered,” meaning you get to prepare topics you actually enjoy—like a book you've read or a project you've created—to discuss during the assessment. It is a recognized end-of-course exam for many visa programmes in Ireland.

The test is task-based with the content chosen by each individual learner and, therefore, can be used to assess learners with different needs and motivations.

It shows prospective employers that the holder of this certificate has successfully completed an English language course.

Who can take this exam?

TIE is inclusive and accessible to everyone. It is suitable for all ages and all English levels, from Beginner (A1) to Advanced (C1). Whether you are on a short-term holiday course or a long-term “Work & Study” programme, TIE is a great fit for proving your communicative ability.

What's in the exam?

The exam consists of two parts: Oral and Written. It assesses your four skills through interaction. You will discuss a book you've read, a news story you've followed, and a personal project you've prepared in your Logbook, alongside spontaneous tasks given by the examiner.
